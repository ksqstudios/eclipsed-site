You feel it before the numbers do.

Sleep debt is real, and it's quietly accumulating. Eclipsed reads your sleep from Health, keeps a rolling total, and shows you exactly how much you're carrying, through color that deepens with the weight.

Download on the App Store iPhone and Apple Watch. One-time purchase, no subscription.

Every night, target minus actual

Eclipsed reads sleep records from the Health app. Your watch already knows; Eclipsed just asks politely.

Each night it takes your target, subtracts what you slept, and adds the difference to a rolling total. Surplus is capped, so a Saturday lie-in can't erase a week of short nights.

The receipts are always there: how long you slept, what you owed, and where the data came from.

Five stages, one glance

The color tells the story before you read the number. Slate when you're rested. Crimson when you've been carrying it for weeks.

Wherever you look last thing at night

On your wrist

The Apple Watch app shows tonight's number and your past 14 nights. Four complication styles keep it on your watch face all day.

Ask Siri

Ask how much sleep you owe, or use the number in a Shortcut. New in 2.1.

Your sleep, read back to you

Every two weeks, Eclipsed writes a short reflection about your recent nights. Not a chart and not a score. A few honest sentences, composed entirely on your device and shown to no one but you. Requires a device with Apple Intelligence.

Unfold for the whole picture

On iPhone Duo, the inner display shows tonight's number with your recent nights beside it. New in 2.1.

What stays yours

Everything.

No accounts. No analytics on you. Sleep data is read from Health and stored on your device. If you use iCloud sync, your history moves between your devices through your own private iCloud database, encrypted and unreadable by anyone else, including us.
